Trading Post

Trading-post progression, tax upgrades, and negotiation bonuses.

Unlock
Player level: 7

Trading Post progression

The Trading Post has separate tax and courage progression. Courage affects negotiation power; tax upgrades define the tax bonus and time limit. Construction of the entrance alone does not bypass tutorial or system requirements.
