Bio

A free-spirited goddess who follows her heart, she adores fine wine, banquets, and all things that bring pleasure. Willful and unrestrained, she answers to no rules, hosting feasts on a whim and bestowing blessings when the mood strikes. Grapes turn to sweet nectar in her palm, and barren lands become lively festival grounds at her arrival. For this, she is seen as the embodiment of indulgence.
