Bio

A homunculus created by Beelsebub with a stone of electric crystal. She is capable of absorbing and converting the electric current her sister Curren releases, for which reason she often stays by her sister's side. She lacks common sense and is very pure at heart. She sometimes expresses her affection through intimate physical contact, often to the embarrassment of others.
