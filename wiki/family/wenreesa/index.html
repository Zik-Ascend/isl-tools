Bio

A solitary traveler, traveling from place to place. Once ostracized by his tribe due to his mixed heritage. He didn't become cold but instead possess a gentle and kind heart. He may seem difficult to get along with at first glance, but he is actually warm-hearted. Not good with words, he silently care for others through his actions and always appear by the side of those in need, having saved many travelers in distress.
