Bio

As Amaterasu's sister, Tsukuyomi holds divine powers but prefers to paint in her room. She once turned her artwork into a moving animation and projected it onto the moon, which unexpectedly charmed the people of Touno Island. She decided to broadcast her artwork on full moon nights. Yet, despite her artistic talent, she's poor at self-care, and often relies on her sister's care.
