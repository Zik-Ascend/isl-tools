Bio

A snow leopard girl from the Northrealm, very timid and often scared to tears. She has strong skills for surviving in the snow-capped mountains, but her considerable timidity has prevented her from passing her tribe's rite of passage into adulthood. Later, she encountered supportive companions and guidance that helped her gather courage for the first time.
