Bio

The goldfish princess who controls luck in Penglai. She shows unlimited curiosity for every new thing she sees in the world outside Penglai and is a master of Penglai's theurgy, which she uses to bring good fortune to others. However, overusing it can deplete her spiritual power, weakening her. It is in those moments when only the finest Longjing Tea can restore her.
