🃏 Profiteer Card - Callus Tips

Tips for "Profiteer Card" Event

I personally don't enjoy the card game so I came up with a few strats to get it over with quickly. First of all I play against NPC as they are stupid and give me a higher chance to win. Just start the game without inviting anyone and NPCs will be automatically added. Now my main strat is to avoid the game in it's entirety, by just leaving as soon as the matchmaking is complete. The AI will keep playing for me in the background and I noticed it to result in about 30% win rate. Just start a game whenever you are online and you don't really have to play at all.

But what if you do want to play?

Now to the game itself:

The developers were kind enough to provide you with an indicator how many cards are left and how likely they are to be one of the profiteer cards.

Early game (<30% chance)

In the beginning try to collect as many cards as possible. The chance of getting a profiteer are low. Use the blue cards (Divine, Discern) if you want to be more careful or don't have an expell card yet. If another player got the profiteer they could place it towards the top of the pile. A shuffle card is a good move here. There is no point in keeping shuffle cards for the late game. Keep the red cards (skip, manipulate) for the late game instead.

Late game (>40% chance)

As the game progresses the chance of getting the profiteer increases. You should have a decent number of cards by now. If you have any blue cards left, get rid of them one by one. You can use manipulate on players with few cards to knock them out. Use skip if the chance gets too high. If you are out of useful cards use swap on the player with the most cards.

General tips

  • You don't need to be first, you need to last as long as possible. So always target the weakest player.
  • When placing the profiteer back into the deck, choose the location for the weakest player to pick it up next. The NPCs will usually use random, but I like to check/shuffle anyways.
  • When using a blue card (especially Divine) and seeing a profiteer, keep the position in mind and count it down. That way you don't need to use blue cards as frequent.
