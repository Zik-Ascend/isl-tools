TypeGuideDate Added2026-09-08

IMPORTANT: Some phases of Touno have 4/8 sakuramochi per 188/988 crystal pack. This was written under the assumption they all have 6/12 per pack. Double check your actual event ingame and adjust your crystal spending accordingly.

go for strolls like this

  1. 500 stroll, 6 shard (6/100)
  2. 1000 stroll, 13 shard (19/100)
  3. 2650 stroll, 35 shard (54/100)
  4. 3800 stroll, 50 shard (104/100)

+324 mochi each touno from 188 & 988 packs

touno 1: 0 mochi spent, 324 mochi owned touno 2: 100 mochi spent, 548 mochi owned touno 3: 430 mochi spent, 442 mochi owned touno 4: 660 mochi spent, 106 mochi owned

so you have about 106 mochi of wiggle room
