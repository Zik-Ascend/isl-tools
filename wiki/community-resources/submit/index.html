Community Resources

Submit a Community Resource

Want your project, guide, tips, calculator, data tool, or another helpful Isekai Slow Life resource listed on the wiki? Send a submission request for review.

Email a resource submission Back to Community Resources

Send your request

Use the email button above, or send the information manually to:

reportissue@zik-ascend-isl-wiki.win

Submissions are curated before being listed. For wiki-hosted guides, you can paste the guide text directly into the email and attach any images you want included.

What can be submitted?

  • Strategy guides, event tips, and reference material.
  • Calculators, spreadsheets, databases, and other community tools.
  • Leaderboard, data-analysis, or research projects.
  • Wiki-hosted written guides when there is no external page to link.

Include enough information to identify the author and resource clearly. Social links are optional and are only shown when the author wants them displayed.
