TypeTips and tricksDate Added2026-09-07

This event heavily relies on team work.

  1. You get ranked by the achieved level, so focus on getting deeper into the dungeon ignoring all the other distractions like minibosses and chests.
  2. Establish in the beginning who has the highest power. That person will be on last hit duty for minibosses.
  3. If you forget where the next winged key is, help uncover the exit
  4. In general screenshot locations of keys/orbs so you remember them
  5. Leave the last orb visible without picking it up for the next fox to pick up
  6. If you stumble upon acid keep it for later.
  7. If you stumble upon a chest use your free open and report it with the megaphone 📣 check regularly to help open discovered chests.
  8. If you stumble upon a little one who is too strong to defeat leave it for the others.
  9. On the last day use your acid to bring down the little ones from weakest to toughest. Only bring them down to the power of your "last-hitter". That person then goes around killing all the little ones and you get the maximum number of loot.
