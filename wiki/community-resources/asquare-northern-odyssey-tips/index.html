Story Mode by ISL discord

  • Do manual as much as possible and not sweep
  • Clear everything on a floor before moving on

Endless Mode by Lyn

  • Do manual as much as possible and not sweep (although slight difference)
  • Keep stone ❄️ for boss level
  • Activate bombs 💣 before killing any monster
  • search the path of the explosion before detonating it, in case there is a Ice Fruit Monster in the way
  • Depending on how far you want to go. I personally don't clear every monster, but try to search for mostly 2 breads in the stage, and open as much space (without killing too much monster) before moving on
  • as far as I remember the marked fields never had monsters in them
