GameplayEvent Rules11 sections
Solo Challenge Chapters

◆ Each chapter consists of multiple levels; challenge them in order starting from Chapter 1.

◆ Starting a chapter costs Sleuth Magnifiers; the amount varies by chapter.

◆ Clear all levels within a chapter to complete it and unlock the next.

◆ If you fail to clear a chapter level, the progress resets. After this, you must use Sleuth Magnifiers to unlock the chapter again and restart it from the first level.

◆ A portion of the Sleuth Magnifiers is refunded if the chapter fails.

※ Chapter 1 Refund: 10

※ Chapter 2 Refund: 15

※ Chapter 3 Refund: 15

※ Chapter 4 Refund: 20

※ Chapter 5 Refund: 20

※ Chapter 6 Refund: 20

Solo Challenge Levels

◆ Find all the items listed within the time limit to clear a level.

◆ Clearing a level grants its rewards and adds one share to the Chapter Reward pool. When you clear the chapter, you receive all the rewards accumulated from its levels.

◆ If you fail to clear a level, the Chapter Reward pool resets.

Solo Challenge Hints

◆ Use the Hint function to locate the first item on the current list quickly.

◆ Some levels provide one free hint at the start.

◆ Each use of a Pocket Watch for extra time also grants one hint.

◆ Hints are only valid for the current level.

Pass

◆ Consuming Sleuth Magnifiers grants Pass Points at a 1:1 ratio.

◆ Rewards can be claimed once your Pass Points reach specific milestones. Purchase the Pass to unlock premium rewards.

Co-op Challenge

◆ Team up with another Transcender for Co-op Challenges. Clearing levels in the Co-op Challenges earns you Synergy Score, which counts toward the leaderboard.

◆ Matchmaking: You will be randomly matched with another Transcender seeking a teammate.

◆ Invite: You can also invite a specific player to team up.

Co-op Challenge Limits

◆ There is a fixed number of Co-op Challenge attempts daily. Once you use them all, you can't play in this mode for the rest of the day

◆ Attempts reset at 00:00 daily. Unused attempts do not carry over to the next day.

Co-op Challenge Levels

◆ During the event, the Co-op Challenge scene changes daily at 00:00.

Synergy Score

◆ Synergy Score is the score earned by a team upon clearing a level.

◆ Synergy Score = Item Score + Team Accuracy Score + Team Speed Score.

◆ Item Score = Sum of points from all items found.

※ Individual Item Points = Base Points x Score Multiplier at the moment the item is found.

※ Base Points = 100.

※ Multiplier: Increases when finding items in quick succession, and decreases if no items are found for a while.

◆ Team Accuracy Score = Initial Accuracy Score - (Total Mistakes x Penalty per mistake).

※ Initial Accuracy Score = 2000.

※ Penalty per mistake = 200.

※ Team Accuracy Score cannot drop below 0.

◆ Team Speed Score = Remaining Seconds x Points per second.

※ Points per second = 200.

Daily Co-op Rewards

◆ Reach Synergy Score milestones to claim rewards. Progress toward Synergy Score rewards resets daily at 00:00, along with the rewards.

Co-op Leaderboard

◆ Each team's highest daily synergy score determines its rank on the Daily Leaderboard, and the sum of all daily synergy scores determines the team's rank on the Overall Leaderboard.

◆ Ranking rewards are distributed based on the Overall Leaderboard after the event ends.

◆ Players receive rewards based on their highest-ranking team among all the teams with which they played in this mode.

Reward Period

◆ A 24-hour reward claim period will be available after the event ends. Don't forget to claim your rewards on time during this period.

◆ Unused Sleuth Magnifiers and Pocket Watches are removed after the event ends.
