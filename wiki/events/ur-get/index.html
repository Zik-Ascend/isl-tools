Event Rules

◆ During the event, you can complete tasks to obtain prayer coins, which can be exchanged for selectable fragments and character fragments.

◆ You can also purchase bundles to obtain a large amount of prayer coins.

◆ Prayer coins obtained during the event will be retained; all these prayer coins can be used across multiple event periods.

◆ This event features four themes: Touno Island Story, Water Fight Competition, Penglai Immortal, and Sandtopia Pilgrimage. Each phase offers fellow and family fragments tied to its theme.

Event Tasks

◆ During the event, you can complete limited tasks to obtain prayer coins.

◆ There are two types of tasks: daily tasks and achievement tasks. Daily tasks refresh every day, while achievement tasks remain unchanged throughout the entire event.

◆ Completing tasks automatically grants Pass EXP. Accumulate EXP to obtain rewards. Completing a daily task grants 5 Pass EXP, while an achievement task grants 100 Pass EXP.

◆ Unclaimed task rewards will not be retained. Please remember to claim them before the event ends.

◆ Task completion progress is not retained; progress resets whenever a new event period begins.

Exchange Shop

◆ Spend prayer coins to exchange for prizes in the Exchange Shop. Note that fragments for UR Fellows and UR Family share the same purchase limit.

◆ The Exchange Shop has levels from 1 to 8. Each exchange grants Shop EXP. As the Shop Level increases, more rewards become available for exchange.

◆ The Shop Level resets whenever a new event period begins.

Item-Selection

◆ Selectable fragments can be obtained through Pass rewards and Exchange Shop rewards.

◆ Use selectable fragments to exchange for Fellow/Family fragments from all four themes (SR excluded).

◆ Selectable fragments will be retained after the event ends.

Privilege Shop

◆ During the Prayer to the Heavens event, UR Fellow/Family fragments of the current theme will appear in the Privilege Shop every Monday.

Limited-Time Exchange

◆ During the event, you can spend privilege coins to exchange for UR Fellow and UR Family fragments of the current theme at a great discount.

◆ Purchase limits for the Limited-Time Exchange remain unchanged throughout the event.

◆ You must purchase one of the Benefits Cards to unlock the Limited-Time Exchange.
