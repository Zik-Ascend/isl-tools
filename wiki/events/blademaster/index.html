Special Instructions

◆ This event will not be repeated. All event items will be cleared or converted into other in-game items after the event ends. All task progress and draw guarantee progress within the event will be cleared.

Event Overview

◆ During the event, you can consume Action Power to dispatch adventurers to complete commissions, earning Commission Points and Adventurer Team EXP.

Adventurers

◆ During the event, all your Fellows (excluding crossover characters) can participate in commissions as adventurers. Crossover characters in this period will be unlocked as adventurers as the event progresses.

◆ Each adventurer has three attributes: combat, negotiation, and exploration. Initial values of these attributes vary among adventurers.

◆ Non-event crossover adventurers will receive bonuses for the three attributes based on Fellow Power.

Commissions

◆ During the event, you will receive various commission tasks. You can dispatch adventurers to form teams to handle these tasks.

◆ A maximum of 3 adventurers can be dispatched for each commission; adventurers cannot participate in multiple commissions at once.

◆ Each commission has different attribute requirements. When the three attributes of the adventurer team exceed the requirements, the highest rating is achieved, earning the most Commission Points.

◆ At the start of the event, you can only take 1 commission at a time. As the adventurer team's level improves, you can handle up to 5 commissions at once.

◆ Each time you dispatch adventurers for a commission, 1 point of Action Power is consumed. Action Power recovers 3 points every 12 hours and can also be restored using items.

Adventurer Team Level

◆ When you get enough Adventurer Team EXP, the adventurer team will level up.

◆ After the adventurer team levels up, you will receive generous rewards and unlock new crossover characters as your adventurers.

Commission Points Achievements

◆ After obtaining enough Commission Points, you can claim generous rewards in the Commission Points Achievements, including this event's exclusive SSR+ characters, UR artifacts, Swordsman Draw Tickets, and more.

◆ Besides completing commission tasks, you can get Commission Points through Bundles.

Event Pass

◆ Claim rewards by reaching certain Pass Point milestones. Purchasing the event pass unlocks advanced rewards, including Swordsman Draw Tickets.

◆ After purchasing the event pass, the number of commissions available will increase.
