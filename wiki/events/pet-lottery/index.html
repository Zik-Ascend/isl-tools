GameplayEvent Rules4 sections
Contract a Legendary Familiar

◆ The Contract a Legendary Familiar event consists of Fruit Gacha, Familiar Hall, and Toy Table.

◆ In Contract a Legendary Familiar event, Transcenders can use Fruit Tokens at the Fruit Gacha to draw Contract Fruits. Use Contract Fruits in the Familiar Hall to feed monsters to obtain their fragments. After you collected all the fragments, the monsters will become your familiar.

Fruit Gacha

Transcenders can use Fruit Tokens at the Fruit Gacha to draw Contract Fruits.

Fruit Tokens can be obtained by purchasing bundles or by participating in the Toy Table.

Familiar Hall

◆ Transcenders can use Contract Fruits in the Familiar Hall to feed monsters and get their fragments.

◆ Feeding monsters to get their fragments; collecting a certain number of fragments will make the monster your familiar.

◆ Feed monsters a certain number of Contract Fruits to get the monsters' SP version.

◆ Transcenders can still consume Contract Fruits to feed desired monsters after the event ends.

Toy Table

◆ Transcenders can craft toys that monsters like at the Toy Table and earn points.

◆ After getting enough points, Transcenders can claim rewards such as Fruit Tokens.

◆ After the event ends, rewards will be distributed based on total points at the Toy Table.
