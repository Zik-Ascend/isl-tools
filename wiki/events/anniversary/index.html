Event Rules

◆ The anniversary event includes two parts: Ceremony Expo and Ceremony Photograph.

Souvenir

◆ During the event, complete specific Ceremony Expo stages to claim player Figure.

◆ If the tasks are completed at the end of the event, the unclaimed souvenir will be sent to Transcenders via email after the event ends..

Ceremony Points

◆ During the event, Transcenders can obtain ceremony points through Special Expo.

◆ Accumulate ceremony points to claim corresponding rewards.

◆ Unclaimed rewards will be sent via mails after the event ends.

Special Expo

◆ Transcenders can enter Special Expo during the event.

◆ Each stage in Special Expo has 5 difficulties for Transcenders to challenge.

◆ Complete Special Expo stages to claim corresponding achievement rewards.

Limited Shops

Transcenders can get Ceremony-Limited Shops through Event Gacha.

Server Heat

The Server Heat = the sum of Ceremony Points of all Transcenders within the server.

Cumulate total Ceremony Points to claim corresponding rewards.
