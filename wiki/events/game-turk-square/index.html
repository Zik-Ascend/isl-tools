Event Rules

◆ During the event, Transcenders can use Shepherd Tickets to participate in the game. Clear the flocks by moving sheep left and right, and prevent them from reaching the top of the pasture.

◆ In the 8x10 pasture, Transcenders can move sheep flocks left and right. When a row of grass is filled with sheep, the flock in that row will be scattered and sent back to the pen.

◆ If a sheep flock reaches the top of the pasture, they will successfully escape and the game will end in failure.

◆ Starting a new game will consume one Shepherd Ticket. Two free Shepherd Tickets are given out daily during the event period. Additional tickets can be purchased in the Crystal Shop.

◆ Shepherd Dogs will appear in the pasture. When a shepherd dog is cleared, the adjacent sheep flock will also be cleared.

◆ Little Bells can separate a designated sheep flock, while Large Bells can separate the 3-sheep flocks into 3 independent sheep and separate 4-sheep flocks into two 2-sheep flocks. Deluxe Carrots can clear all sheep flocks of the same color in the current pasture.

Gift Boxes

◆ Clear gift boxes on the pasture to obtain item rewards.

Ranking Rewards

◆ Clearing the sheep during the event will earn you a certain amount of scores. After the event ends, rewards will be distributed based on the highest score achieved in a single round.

Achievement Rewards

◆ Achieving a certain score during the event will earn Transcenders corresponding achievement rewards.

Trial Mode

◆ In Trial Mode, it won't cost Shepherd Tickets for Transcenders to take part in the game.

◆ Transcenders can obtain 5 Little Bells, Large Bells, and Deluxe Carrots at the beginning of each round. These items can only be used in Trial Mode.

◆ The progress in Trial Mode won't be saved. A new round will begin each time Transcenders enter Trial Mode.

◆ Transcenders won't obtain any reward in Trial Mode. The scores obtained in Trial Mode won't be used for achievement rewards or for rankings.
