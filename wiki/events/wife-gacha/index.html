Special Notes

◆ At event start, you must choose a Family Member to adventure with. This choice is locked for the event duration.

◆ After selection, the draw pool, exploration map, and event story will correspond to the chosen Family Member.

Exploration Map

◆ Initially, the entire scene is covered in fog. As you move across the map, the surrounding fog gradually clears.

◆ You can spend stamina to gather interactable objects on the map, receiving rewards upon completion.

◆ Monsters may block the path. Dispatch Family Members to defeat them and proceed.

◆ Spending stamina grants EXP. Meeting the required amount boosts family power on map.

◆ Stamina recovers automatically over time (1 per 6 minutes).

Main Task

◆ Main tasks will guide you in completing the map exploration.

◆ Completing the main tasks will unlock more tasks.

Event Pass

◆ Spending stamina grants Pass Points at a certain ratio.

◆ Claim rewards by reaching certain Pass Point milestones. Purchasing the event pass unlocks premium rewards.

◆ Activating the event Pass grants 10 Holiday Tickets and unlock a special effect: Family Power +600 on map.
