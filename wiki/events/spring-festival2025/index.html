Event Gameplay

◆ The event includes three activities: the Lion Dance lottery, the New Year Flower cultivation, and the collection and exchange of Paper Cuttings.

Spring Festival Luck

◆ Enter the event daily to record your logins in Spring Festival Luck and receive login rewards corresponding to the day.

◆ Besides day rewards, you will also receive random paper cuttings for logging in.

◆ Rewards for missed logins can be obtained by login recovery.

◆ You can recover a login once daily. Each login recovery costs 100 crystals.

◆ Transcenders can select a date to recover a login.

Battle Pass

◆ Exchange paper cuttings to gain event points. Achieving a certain score in the event will improve your Battle Pass level and earn you corresponding level rewards.

◆ Activate the Pass to receive the Festival limited Figure Chest and SSR+ Familiar Brushcat.

◆ Unclaimed Pass rewards will be sent to your mail after the event ends.

Brushcat Star Bundle

◆ After obtaining Familiar Brushcat, the bundles will unlock in sequence.

◆ Transcenders can obtain star-upgrade materials for Brushcat and the SP version of Brushcat from the Star Bundle.

◆ Unclaimed free rewards will be sent to your mail after the event ends.
