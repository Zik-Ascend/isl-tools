Event Gameplay

◆ During the event, spend 1 Ancient Gold for a draw to receive random rewards, including the Magi Token. Tap "Details" to view the prizes.

Magi Token

◆ Magi Tokens can be used to summon a magus or upgrade their skills. Can be obtained through Family Diary or by purchasing pass.

Token Fragment

◆ Token Fragments can be combined into a Magi Token. Can be obtained through Family Diary or by purchasing pass.

Item Conversion

◆ When all Magi's Artifact events end, any unused Ancient Gold will be converted into Token Fragments at a certain ratio.

◆ Use Token Fragments to redeem fragments for a specific Magi Token.
