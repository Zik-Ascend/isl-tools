Event Rules

◆ During the event, players can play the game by merging materials together.

◆ Transcenders can move materials up, down, left, and right in the 4x4 grid to merge lower-level materials into higher-level ones, up to a maximum of level 11.

◆ Each movement consumes 1 Stamina. 10 Stamina are restored every 10 minutes. Transcenders can also drink Special Tea to restore Stamina immediately. The Stamina limit is capped at 500.

◆ Transcenders will unlock Rush Mode after reaching a certain VIP level. Transcenders can switch different speed modes freely. In Normal Mode, each movement consumes 1 stamina and produces Lv. 1 materials; in Quick Mode, each movement consumes 4 Stamina and produces Lv. 3 materials; in Rush mode, each movement consumes 8 stamina and produces Lv. 4 materials. When materials appear in the Quick and Rush mode, Transcenders will obtain extra points and Apothecary Vouchers based on the material level.

◆ Clearance items will clear 1 material. Rearrangement items will sort and reorder all materials according to level from high to low.

Random Rewards

◆ A chest may have chances to appear when a material is produced. Merge the material to obtain the rewards in chest.

◆ Merging materials will reward a small amount of Apothecary Vouchers that can be exchanged in the Exchange Shop for items.

Rank Rewards

◆ Merging materials will reward Transcenders a certain amount of points. After the event ends, Transcenders will be ranked and rewarded according to their total points and highest points in a single round.

Exchange Shop

◆ Apothecary Vouchers can be used to exchange for items. The exchange limits will be reset at 00:00 every day.

◆ After the event ends, unused Apothecary Vouchers will be retained for further exchanges in the next event.

◆ Unused Special Tea, Rearrangement, and Clearance items will be converted into Apothecary Vouchers.
