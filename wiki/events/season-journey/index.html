GameplayEvent Rules1 section
Rank Challenge

◆ The Rank Challenge will be open for 24 days. All Transcenders who have reached level 21 and above can participate.

◆ This challenge will not rerun.

◆ During the challenge, complete tasks to improve your tier and earn corresponding rewards.

◆ You can also gain rewards by checking in during the challenge. Accumulated check-ins will get you corresponding rewards.

◆ Reward claiming period: After the Rank Challenge ends, a 72-hour period will be available to collect rewards. The Rank Challenge content will become unavailable once the entrance to the challenge closes.

◆ All task completion progress and tier progress will be cleared after the challenge ends.

◆ There are four types of rewards in the Rank Challenge: tier rewards, check-in rewards, chest rewards, and score rewards.

◆ The Chest Keys and Challenge Tokens won't be reset after the event ends.
