Event Rules

During the event, cooperate with your family to make family exclusive Xocolate and improve Intimacy with your family.

During the event, consume Crystal Lard to make Chocolate for your family to improve their protective power, and also get Love Tickets to draw Time-Limited Costume for your family.

Collaborative Xocolate

Invited family members will regularly wait to make xocolates during the event. Newly invited family members can also make xocolates during the event.

Completing the xocolate with family members for the first time will increase intimacy. Successfully making family-exclusive chocolates that match the family member's taste will receive additional intimacy rewards.

Transcenders can only receive one reward from each family member.

During the event, Transcenders can invite family members who have already cooperated to make Xocolates again at any time.

Gift Xocolates

Consuming Cacao Butter to make and gift xocolates to family members can improve their blessing power and obtain love tickets.

If you haven't cooperated with family members to make exclusive xocolates, you can only give them imperfect products and cannot gift them with exclusive xocolates.
