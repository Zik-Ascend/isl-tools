Special Notes

◆ During this event, you can obtain anexclusive SSR+ character and improve their rarity to UR★.

◆ This event will not return in the foreseeable future. After it ends, there will be no way of obtaining the event's exclusive characters.

◆ All event items will expire or convert into other items after the event ends, while the task and gacha progress will reset.

Event Overview

◆ During the event, spend Streamer Stamina to assign Fellows to assist in streaming tasks, gain fans, and improve the Stream Level.

Livestream Participation

◆ Participate in the event by assigning your Fellows to complete streaming tasks.

◆ Each fellow has three attributes: Talk, Sing, and Sponsor. Initial values of these attributes vary among Fellows.

◆ Each Fellow will receive attribute bonuses based on their power.

Streaming Tasks

◆ Players will receive various streaming tasks during the event. Form teams of Fellows to handle them.

◆ A maximum of 3 Fellows can be assigned to each task; Fellows cannot participate in multiple tasks at once.

◆ Each task has different attribute requirements. When the three attributes of the assigned Fellow team exceed the requirements, the highest score is achieved, gaining the most fans.

◆ At the start of the event, you can only assign Fellows to 1 streaming task at a time. As the Stream Level improves, you can assign Fellows to handle up to 5 tasks at once.

◆ Each time you assign Fellows to streaming tasks, 1 point of Action Power is consumed. Action Power recovers 3 points every 12 hours.

Stream Level

◆ Getting a certain number of fans will improve the Stream Level.

◆ Obtain rewards by improving the Stream Level.

Fan Count Achievement

◆ Gain fans to obtain generous rewards from Fan Count achievements, including Stream Tickets and event-exclusive artifacts, titles, and so forth.

Livestream Pass

◆ Claim rewards by reaching certain Fan Count milestones. Purchasing the event's pass unlocks advanced rewards, including special Stream Tickets.

◆ After purchasing the event's pass, the number of streaming tasks available will increase.
