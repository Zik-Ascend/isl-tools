Mushroom's Travels

◆During the event, Transcedeners can spend 1 Mushroom Piece to travel once and obtain all item rewards on the route. Spending 10 Mushroom Pieces at a time will grant 1 additional travel chance. It can also consume gold to travel but there is a daily limit for it and the probability to receive rewards is different from using Mushroom Pieces.

◆When the number of Mushroom Pieces consumed reaches a certain number, Transcenders will be guaranteed to receive limited characters and limited costumes from the travel.

◆A new board will unlock for each new Mushroom's Travels. The obtainable limited characters and limited costumes are different on each board. The travel progress and guaranteed rewards status in the same board will be inherited. The travel progress and guaranteed rewards status in different boards are independent.

Mushroom Pieces

◆During the event, Transcenders can obtain Mushroom Pieces through daily tasks and limited bundles. After the event ends, each unused will be kept for Mushroom's Travels in the future.

Event Stories

◆There is a chance for Transcenders to obtain story items during the travels. Collect enough of them to unlock stories and obtain event-limited costumes in the end.

◆In the later Mushroom's Travels, the obtained story items and story unlock progress will be inherited. The story items and story unlock progress in different boards are independent.

Travel Rewards

◆After obtaining the event-limited costumes, there is a chance to obtain reward items. Collect enough of them to claim travel rewards.

◆In the later Mushroom's Travels, the obtained reward items and unlock progress will be inherited. The reward items and unlock progress in different boards are independent.

Board Refresh

◆Use Refresh to rearrange the layout of the board and generate an extra valuable reward on the board. But the positions of starting point, end, and some special squares will not change.
