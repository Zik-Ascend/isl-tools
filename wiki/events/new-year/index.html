New Year Event

◆ The New Year event has begun, featuring Check-In, Omikuji, New Year Visit, and New Year Gacha.

Event Check-In

◆ Login to the game during the event to claim rewards.

Omikuji

◆ During the event, you can draw one Omikuji each day to check your fortune and receive a draw reward.

New Year Visit

◆ Complete New Year Visit tasks during the event to earn coins. Spend these coins to draw rewards. You can view the reward pool via the Details button.

◆ New Year Visit tasks are divided into Personal Tasks and Guild Tasks.

◆ Guild Tasks: When enough guild members complete the task, all guild members can claim that reward.

◆ If members leave or are removed from the guild, the guild’s task progress may drop and revert to an incomplete state.

◆ Each guild task reward can only be claimed once per person. Changing guilds does not allow claiming again.

◆ After the event ends, unclaimed task rewards will be sent by mail. Each unused New Year Visit Coins will be converted into 5 Crystals.

New Year Pass

◆ Complete New Year Visit tasks to earn Pass Points.

◆ When Pass Points reach the required amount, rewards can be claimed. Purchasing the Pass unlocks premium rewards.

◆ After the event ends, unclaimed Pass rewards will be sent by mail.

New Year Gacha

◆ Use 1 New Year Ticket to draw for random rewards, including limited costumes. Tap Details to view the prizes.

◆ Get New Year Tickets through checking in, purchasing pass, purchasing limited bundles.

◆ Duplicate costumes can be used to upgrade their levels.

◆ Reach certain draw counts to claim Progress Rewards.

◆ Each unused New Year Ticket will convert into 200 Crystals after the event ends.

Other Notes

◆ No claim period for this event. Please use your event items in time.
