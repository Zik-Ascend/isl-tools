GameplayEvent Rules5 sections
Lucky Spin

◆ Use 1 Lucky Spin Ticket to draw once. Each draw guarantees 1 reward. Tap Details to view the rewards.

Lucky Spin Tickets

◆ Lucky Spin Tickets can be obtained from Bundles, Pass, and Event Tasks.

◆ Each unused Lucky Spin Ticket will convert into 100 Crystals after the event ends.

Crystal Prize Pool

◆ Each draw adds a certain amount of Crystals to the prize pool.

◆ Each draw has a chance to trigger a Lucky Moment, granting a percentage of the Crystals in the prize pool.

◆ Lucky Crystal rewards come in two tiers (large and small). See the probability preview for details.

◆ Each Lucky Spin draw adds a certain amount of Crystals to the prize pool, capped at 288,888 Crystals.

Snowflake Wish

◆ Use Snowflake Wish Tickets to draw rewards. Each reward in the pool has a limited stock and cannot be drawn once depleted.

Snowflake Wish Tickets

◆ Snowflake Wish Tickets can be obtained from Lucky Spin draws.

◆ Each unused Snowflake Wish Ticket will convert into 300 Crystals after the event ends.
