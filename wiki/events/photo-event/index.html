GameplayEvent Rules4 sections
Photo Taking

◆During the event, Transcenders can consume Roll Film to take photos.

◆Shooting work is divided into four stages: Character Selection, Scene Setting, Photo Taking, and Boudoir Photo.

◆Transcenders can receive a reward for each work. Received rewards will be excluded from the prize pool.

◆Obtaining the grand prize will automatically receive all remaining rewards and enter the next stage.

Photo Rewards

◆Transcenders can obtain family's limited costume in Photo Taking stage.

◆Transcenders can obtain family's limited costume CG in Boudoir Photo stage. After collecting the limited CG, corresponding family's Intimacy and Blessing Power will increase. Collected CG can be viewed in the Gallery.

Theme

◆ A new theme will unlock after each Sepia Portrait Studio opens. Different themes offer different limited costume and limited Boudoir CG. The shooting progress within the same theme will be inherited, and the progress of different themes is not shared.

◆ Some themes include two characters. After selecting this theme, you can switch between the two characters at any time during the event. The two characters do not share shooting progress, and only when both characters have completed the Photo Taking stage, the Boudoir Photo stage will be unlocked.

Repeat Shooting

◆ After completing the Boudoir Photo work, you can choose the current theme again, and you can get more limited costume blueprints.
