GameplayEvent Rules2 sections
Qualifiers

◆ Qualifiers use the default lineup of transcenders.

◆ Winning in Qualifiers grants points; losing deducts points.

◆ Defeating an opponent ranked higher than grants more points. (For every 5 ranks higher, grants 1 extra points. Capped at 5 points.)

◆ The top 32 contestants advance to the Championship.

◆ Each challenge costs a Challenge Attempt. Transcenders receive 1 Challenge Attempt per hour.

◆ Challenge Attempt Cap: 25. Attempts stop regenerating once the cap is reached.

◆ Transcenders can spend Crystals to purchase additional Challenge Attempts when out, up to 100 times per day.

Championship

◆ The Match Bracket is based on the final rankings of transcenders in Qualifiers. They need to progress through the Round of 16, Quarterfinals, Semifinals, and Finals.

◆ Each player has three squads. Each round is best of three games.
