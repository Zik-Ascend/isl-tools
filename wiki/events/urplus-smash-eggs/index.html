Golden Egg Smash

◆ During the event, use Golden Hammers to pull random rewards, including Luck Stars. Tap Details to view the prizes.

◆ Golden Hammers can be obtained through completing tasks and purchasing bundles.

◆ Each round contains nine golden eggs. Smash any egg to claim its reward; only one egg holds a Lucky Star. Once you obtain a given reward, it can't be got again in the round.

◆ Upon getting the Lucky Star, you automatically win all remaining rewards in the round, refreshing the prize pool.

◆ All golden egg rewards will reset after the event ends.

Lucky Stars

◆ Lucky Star can be obtained through smashing the golden eggs or converted from the draw counts that didn't reach the pity draw count in the past UR★ events.

◆ Lucky Stars can be used to redeem UR★ and SSR characters and fragments in Advanced Exchange.
