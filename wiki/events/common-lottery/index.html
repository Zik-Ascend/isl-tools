Event Rules

◆During the event, consume Gift Vouchers Coin to draw in the Gift Tree. You'll receive a random reward including limited Costumes. Tap Details to view rewards content.

◆Each Gift Voucher will be converted into Crystals *100 after the event ends.

Progress Rewards

◆When Transcenders draw certain times, the corresponding Progress Rewards can be claimed.

Costume Return

◆If the Crow's Theatre was opened 60 days ago or earlier, the Theatre Limited costumes blueprints (★3) will be added to the Progress Reward.

Event Rules

During the event, Transcenders can spend 50 Love Tickets to participate in the lottery and get random rewards, including Time-Limited Costumes. Check the reward detail through "Details."

Select the Time-Limited Costume before drawing, and the obtained costumes and fragments will be based on your current selection.

During the event, Transcenders can consume Cacao Butter to make Xocolate for your family and get Love Tickets.

Each unused Love Ticket will be converted into 1 Crystal after the event ends.

Event Rules

◆During the event, consume Valiant Sigil to draw rewards. You'll receive a random reward including Ohterworld Valiants. Tap Details to view rewards content.

◆This event will not rerun. But Transcenders can still get Valiant Insignia and Insignia fragments through other in-game events or functions.
