Activity TypeGameThreeMatchVariations2Duration3 or 4 days

Across all variations

Core Rewards

「Woof Woof Bread」
GameplayEvent Rules4 sections
Event Rules

◆ During the event period, Transcenders can complete customer orders by playing the game and eliminating bread pieces to obtain whole bread.

◆ In the 6x7 grid, Transcenders can move swap pieces left, right, up, and down. When three or more of the same bread pieces have formed a row or column, the bread can be successfully collected and Transcenders will receive points.

◆ Each position swap requires Stamina. At 1x Speed Mode, each swap consumes 1 Stamina.

◆ Once Transcenders' VIP level reaches 5, they can activate 16x Speed Mode. In this mode, Stamina consumed and the score obtained will be multiplied by 16.

◆ At the beginning of the event, Transcenders will receive 120 Stamina (1 Stamina is restored every 5 minutes). Honey Black Tea can be used to quickly restore Stamina.

Customer Orders

◆ Transcenders can collect the required food and complete customer orders by swapping the positions of bread pieces.

◆ Transcenders can obtain coupons through each collection of bread pieces. They can be used to exchange goods in the Exchange Shop.

Rank Rewards

◆ Transcenders can earn points each time they collect bread. After the event ends, players will be ranked and rewarded according to their total points.

Exchange Shop

◆ Coupons can be used to exchange for items. The exchange limits will be reset at 00:00 every day.

◆ After the event ends, unused coupons will retained for further exchanges in the next time the event opens.

◆ Unused Honey Black Tea will be converted into coupons.
