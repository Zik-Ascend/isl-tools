GameplayEvent Rules5 sections
Dice Rolls

◆The dice rolls will be replenished at 00:00 every day. If the number of remaining dice exceeds or equals 5 rolls, it will not be replenished.

Square Rewards

◆When the piece stays in a square, the reward of that square will be obtained.

Returning Rewards

◆After completing each circle, a reward of starting point can be claimed.

Fairy Blessing

◆When the piece stays in a square blessed by a fairy, the rewards of other blessed squares that are connected will be obtained as well.

The Crystal Realm

◆Stay on the swirling square to teleport to the Crystal Realm. It doesn't cost dices to move in the Crystal Realm.
