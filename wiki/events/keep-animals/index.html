GameplayEvent Rules5 sections
Flower Growth and Pollination

◆ Spend Flower Tickets or Crystals to purchase Lv. 1 to Lv. 3 flower seedlings. Up to 6 flowers can be planted simultaneously.

◆ After obtaining seedlings, they will have a growth countdown. Once the countdown ends, the seedlings become flowers. Fertilizer can be used to shorten the growth time.

◆ When you have two flowers of the same species, you can pollinate them using Pollination Potion with a chance to obtain higher-level seedlings.

◆ After pollination, flowers enter a resting period, during which they can't be pollinated again.

Fertilizer and Pollination Potion

◆ Using 1 Fertilizer can accelerate seedling growth time by 10 minutes. Obtain Fertilizer by completing daily tasks, using the event's exchange shop, and buying Value Packs or crystal packs.

◆ Pollinating flowers requires Pollination Potion, with higher-level flowers consuming more potions. Obtain Pollination Potion by completing daily tasks, using the event shop, and buying Value Packs or Crystal Packs.

Exchange Tickets

◆ Flower Tickets can be obtained by selling flowers and completing daily tasks.

◆ Get various rewards by spending Flower Tickets in the event's shop.

Daily Tasks and Achievements

◆ Completing daily tasks earns Pollination Potion, Fertilizer, and Flower Tickets. Daily tasks reset at 0:00 every day.

◆ Completing achievement tasks earns various item rewards.

Other Notes

◆ This event has no reward claim period. Unclaimed task rewards after the event ends will be sent via mail.

◆ After the event ends, any unused Flower Tickets, Fertilizers, or Pollination Potions will be removed. Please make sure to use them in time.
