Basic Rules

◆The Profiteer Card deck consists of 41 cards, including 3 Profiteer cards, 6 Expel cards, 4 Reverse cards, 4 Demand cards, 4 Discern cards, 4 Skip cards, 4 Shuffle cards, 4 Divine cards, 4 Swap cards, 4 Manipulate cards.

◆The game need 4 Transcenders to play. At the beginning of the game, each Transcender is dealt 5 cards.

◆After the round starts, the Transcenders play the cards in counterclockwise direction. During their own turn, Transcenders can play their cards or draw a card to end their turn.

Card Description

◆Profiteer Card: If this card is drawn, Transcenders need to Expel it immediately or they'll be eliminated.

◆Expel Card: Expel the Profiteer Card and put it back to the Draw pile.

◆Reverse Card: Reverse the direction of play.

◆Demand Card: Play the card to target a Transcender and obtain a random card from them.

◆Discern Card: Check the top 3 cards on the Draw pile.

◆Skip Card: Skip your turn without drawing or playing any card.

◆Shuffle Card: Shuffle the Draw pile.

◆Divine Card: Play the card to check the position of the first Profiteer Card.

◆Swap Card: Play the card to target a Transcender to swap all your cards with them.

◆Manipulate Card: Play the card to target a Transcender to act 2 times in a row, and skip your turn.

Scoring and Winning

◆During each game, if a Profiteer Card is drawn, Transcenders need to use Expel Card to expel it immediately. Otherwise, they'll lose the game and is eliminated. Remainig Transcenders will keep playing until there's only one Transcender left.

◆During the event, Transcenders will obtain scores after completing each game. The first eliminated Transcender will obatin 100 scores; the second will obtain 200 scores; the third will obtain 300 scores, and the last Transcender will win the game and obtain 400 scores.

◆The victory count of the winning Transcender and the third eliminated one will plus 1.

Achievement Rewards

◆Achievement tasks are divided into Achieve Victory, and Scores Obtained.

◆Progress of Achieve Victory and Scores Obtained will keep cumulating until the event ends.

◆Claim the rewards after completing the tasks. Unclaimed rewards will be sent to Transcenders via mails.

Beginner Game

◆In Transcenders' first game, they'll be matched with NPC opponents.

◆Winning in the beginner game will not earn any score or increase the number of victories.
