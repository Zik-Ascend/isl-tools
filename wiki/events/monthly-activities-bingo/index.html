Event Tasks

◆ During the event, tasks unlock day by day. Complete them to obtain glues for fixing the diary.

Diary Fix

◆ Use 1 Ordinary Glue to fix a random piece of the diary. If the randomly selected diary piece has already been fixed, you will receive 1 Super Glue.

◆ Use 10 Super Glues to fix a specified piece of the diary.

◆ There are 3 pages of the diary that need to be fixed during the event. They need to be repaired in order.

◆ After the event ends, the remaining Ordinary Glues and Super Glues will be collected. Each Ordinary Glue will be converted into 10 Crystals and will be sent to Transcenders via email.

Fix Rewards

◆ When a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of the diary page's grid is fixed, the corresponding Fix Reward will be earned.

◆ Fixing a complete page of the diary will get you the Final Fix Reward. The Final Fix Reward for the second page is a costume.
