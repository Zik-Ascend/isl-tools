Event Rules

◆ Build! A Leisurely Countryside Village event includes three parts: Special Expo, Draw, and Fluffy Ranch.

Event Points

◆ During the event, Transcenders can earn event points by completing the Special Expo, playing Fluffy Ranch, and harvesting crops.

◆ Accumulate Event Points to claim corresponding rewards.

◆ Unclaimed rewards will be sent via mails after the event ends.

Special Expo

◆ Transcenders can enter Special Expo during the event.

◆ Each stage in Special Expo has 5 difficulties for Transcenders to challenge.

◆ Complete Special Expo stages to claim corresponding achievement rewards.

Farm

◆ Watch daily stories to unlock farm.

◆ The production efficiency of each farm varies. Please harvest the crops before reaching the storage limit.

◆ Crops produced by the farm are automatically converted into event points.
