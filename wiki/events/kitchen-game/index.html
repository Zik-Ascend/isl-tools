Food Truck Management

◆ While playing Food Truck Management, use your Ceremony Tokens to purchase ingredients to prepare dishes at the Cook Station. Once you sell the dishes, you'll earn more Ceremony Tokens. Get as many tokens as possible to redeem lots of rewards!

◆ The Cook Stations on the Food Truck unlock gradually over time. Some require spending Crystal to unlock.

◆ Each Cook Station on the Food Truck can only produce one dish at a time.

◆ Different dishes take varying amounts of time to prepare. Use items to accelerate the preparation time.

◆ After preparing a dish, you must collect it and place it in the Deli to sell it.

◆ The selling price of dishes changes randomly every 2 hours.

◆ Selling large quantities of the same dish will cause a price drop, lasting until the next price refresh.

Helping Friends

◆ You can visit a friend's Food Truck to assist in preparing dishes. Each assistance reduces the preparation time by 20 minutes.

◆ Only one assistance is allowed per dish.

◆ There is a daily limit to the number of times you can assist friends or be assisted by friends.

Reward Period

◆ There will be a 24-hour claim period for rewards after the event ends. Please redeem your rewards in the exchange shop during this period; once it ends, the shop will close, and Food Truck Coins will expire.

◆ Once the reward period starts, unfinished dishes will be removed (with no rewards received for them).

◆ Once the reward period starts, all dishes in the Deli will be automatically sold at the lowest price.
