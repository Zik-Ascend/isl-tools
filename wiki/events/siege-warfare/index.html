Basic Gameplay

◆ During the Siege Warfare ~Showdown~ event, each guild will compete in Drakenberg simulated battlefield to earn more points.

◆ Transcenders enter the battlefield as part of their guild. Each guild is assigned to 4 bases initially.

◆ Occupy more bases or higher-tier bases to earn more points at daily settlement, improving event rank.

Event System

◆ Guilds are grouped by total power. Guilds ranked 1-16 are Master level, 17-32 are Expert level, and 33-48 are Elite level.

◆ Each match groups 4 guilds together to compete.

◆ No elimination rules apply in the competition. Eligible guilds may join anytime during the event.

◆ The event schedule is as follows: ※Grouping Phase: Monday 00:00 --- Monday 21:00 ※Preparation Phase: Monday 21:00 --- Monday 21:30 ※Official Matches: Tuesday to Sunday. Pre-Battle Stage: daily {time1}---{time2}. Battle Stage: daily {time3}---{time4}. Truce Stage: daily {time4}---{time1} the next day.

◆ The guild can only participate if there are Transcenders who have recently logged into the game before the end of the grouping stage. Once the guild qualifies to participate, its members will also be able to participate. Members who join the guild on the same day can participate immediately.

Bases

◆ Bases are divided into 3 tiers: Low-Tier, Mid-Tier, and High-Tier.

◆ When dispatching squads to bases, the minimum number of fellows required for each squad is related to the base tier and will increase with the server's opening days.

◆ Base tiers will determine the points and rewards obtained from occupied bases. Claiming the daily Base Rewards requires consuming at least 1 Action Point of a fellow.

◆ After the end of each day's Battle Stage, the points for the day will be calculated based on the tier and number of bases occupied by each guild in the group: ※Low-Tier Base: 1 point. ※Mid-Tier Base: 2 points. ※High-Tier Base: 4 points.

◆ At the end of each event, rankings are based on the guild’s total daily points during the event, with rewards distributed according to rank.

Wage War

◆ During the Pre-Battle Stage, the guild president, vice president, and elite members can wage war on other bases.

◆ Only bases adjacent to guild-held bases can be targeted, provided no other guild has already waged war on them.

◆ If your guild hasn't occupied any bases, you can wage war on any Low-Tier Base.

◆ To wage war on a Mid-Tier Base, your guild must occupy at least 2 Low-Tier bases or 1 High-Tier Base. To wage war on a High-Tier Base, your guild must occupy at least 1 Mid-Tier Base.

◆ During the competition, each guild has 3 chances to wage war per day. Chances reset daily.

Garrison

◆ Guild members can dispatch squads to guard their occupied bases during both the Pre-Battle Stage and Battle Stage.

◆ Squads assigned to defense a base cannot be manually recalled and will only be removed automatically upon defeat.

◆ Fellows in a dispatched squad are considered in battle and cannot be sent to guard other bases. When the squad is withdrawn, Fellows return to standby status.

◆ When the Truce Stage begins, all squads garrisoning bases are removed, and guild members can redeploy their defenses the next day.

Occupying Bases

◆ During the Battle Stage, guild members can send squads to attack bases their guild has waged war on.

◆ Defeat all garrisoned squads in the opponent's base to occupy it.

◆ At the end of the Battle Stage, your guild will successfully hold the base as long as at least one defensive squad remains.

◆ After defeating all opponent squads, your remaining squads will automatically garrison the base.

Launching a Counterattack

◆ A guild whose base has been occupied can issue a counterattack command during the Counterattack Protection Period (15 minutes from the time of occupation). After the phase ends, squads can be dispatched for a counterattack.

◆ During the counterattack, the roles of offense and defense are reversed. Defeat all defensive squads deployed there to reclaim the base.

◆ Once the base is reclaimed, the opponent can no longer launch a counterattack.

◆ Only the guild president, vice president, and elite members can initiate a counterattack command. If not issued before the deadline, the chance to counterattack is lost for that day.

Fellows and Squads

◆ Your fellows’ power is locked at the end of each day’s Pre-Battle Stage.

◆ After the Pre-Battle Stage ends, any changes to the fellows' power will not apply to that day’s Siege Warfare.

◆ Each fellow starts with 2 Action Points per day. The points will not recover over time, but will reset to 2 points at the end of the daily Truce Stage.

◆ Assigning a fellow to an offensive or defensive squad and deploying them to a base consumes 1 Action Point.

◆ Fellows with zero Action Points cannot join any squad.

◆ Transcenders can deploy squads through Dispatch Fellows and Preset Squads. ※Dispatch Fellows: Transcenders can manually select fellows and filter based on type, or set up quick formations to rapidly dispatch multiple squads. ※Preset Squads: Transcenders can pre-edit squads and deploy them instantly during dispatch.

◆ Guild president, vice president, and elite members can adjust the order of squads deployed in a base, except for the first 3 non-adjustable squads.

Battle

◆ The offensive and defensive squads engage in 1v1 battles, with fellows from each squad fighting in turn.

◆ Fellows deal damage based on their power, and the stronger side wins. The winning fellow does not recover HP, and any damage taken carries over to the next battle.

◆ When all fellows in a squad are defeated, the squad automatically withdraws from the base and the next squad enters the battle.

◆ There is a 1‑second interval between each squad battle.

◆ The offensive squad can gain an Offensive Buff during battle, which boosts the Fellow Power by 10%. The buff weakens with each consecutive victory. For every KO by the squad, the buff decreases by 1% until it reaches zero or the squad is defeated.

◆ The winning squad will receive a Winning Streak Debuff, which will decrease the power of the squad's fellows. The debuff increases with each consecutive victory. For every KO by the squad, Fellow Power decreases by 1% until it reaches the limit or the squad is defeated.

Leaderboard

◆ Guilds are ranked daily from highest to lowest in their corresponding groups based on their accumulated score. Transcenders can claim rewards during the reward period. The individual MVP of the ranking will be determined based on the player's KO count during the event.

◆ Ranking rewards differ for Master, Expert, and Elite levels.

◆ To claim rewards, you must have participated in the current event by consuming at least 1 Action Point.

◆ The top 3 Transcenders with the highest KO count during the daily Battle Stage will be featured on the MVP list, divided into "Offense" and "Defense" categories and ranked based on their KO counts.
