Bio

An ancient dragon with power great enough to destroy entire city states. Her dream is to become a Great Evil Dragon that's worth a billion bounty. Unfortunately, her naturally kind heart and her habit of treating a ridiculous third rate novel as a sacred guide often cause her "world shaking evil deeds" to fail halfway or even accidentally turn into acts of goodwill. Completely unaware of this irony, she remains cheerfully self satisfied and continues striving tirelessly toward a goal she is destined never to achieve.
