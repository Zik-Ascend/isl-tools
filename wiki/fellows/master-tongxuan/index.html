Bio

The owner of a rural apothecary who was once a famous medicine immortal in Penglai, whose concocted pills are worth a fortune. However, Tongxuan himself is not ambitious in this regard. Rather than being a revered immortal, he longs for a leisurely and carefree hermit life. He desires nothing and acts only according to his will. Not even a giant carriage could move him when he doesn't want to do something.

Source Penglai Immortal Internal: 3042
