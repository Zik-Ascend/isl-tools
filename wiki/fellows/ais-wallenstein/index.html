Bio

An adventurer from the Otherworld, with blonde hair and golden eyes, she is as beautiful as a god. Mastering sword skills among the best, she is known as the "Sword Princess". However, she's a bad drinker, always going on rampage after getting drunk, so don't let her drink too much. Potato, a specialty of Orario, is her favorite, and her personality is basically simple and naive.
