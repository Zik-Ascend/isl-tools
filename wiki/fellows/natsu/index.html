Bio

A wizard from another world and a proud member of the guild Fairy Tail. Raised by the Fire Dragon, Igneel, Natsu mastered the Dragon Slayer Magic taught directly by his adoptive father. Natsu is straightforward and quick-tempered, but his care for his friends runs deeper than anyone's. His blazing spirit and loyalty have earned him the guild's complete trust.
