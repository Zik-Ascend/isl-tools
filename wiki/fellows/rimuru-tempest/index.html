Bio

A Slime from another world possesses a very gentle personality and embraces pacifism. Enthusiastic about fun activities and culinary culture, it is reputed to have been dedicated to enhancing entertainment and cultural levels in its original world. Despite its cute appearance, it wields overwhelming power and commands many followers. Those who underestimate it are unlikely to meet a good end.
