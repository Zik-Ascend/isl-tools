Bio

A lively girl who descended from another world. It is said that she took great pleasure in resolving the troubles of ordinary folks in the world she originally came from. A stroke of luck led her to this world. She was enchanted by Fifi’s stall and threw herself into part-time work with the goal of dominating the entire stall street.

Source Obtained in the [Café LycoReco Crossing into Isekai] crossover event. Internal: 83514
