Bio

The Judge of Hell, who can judge whether a soul is guilty. They'll bring sinners into hell for re-education until their souls are purified before being released. Bel and Ross often have different opinions, while the reserved Kerr, as the final arbiter, can absorb both views and make the most justified judgment. The three sisters' contrasting personalities complement each other, leading to their outstanding achievements in their work.

Source Guild Trade Festival Internal: 3008
