Bio

A swordsman in the Demon Slayer Corps and one of Tanjiro's peers. He is combative and loves to rush headfirst into battle. He uses the Beast Breathing technique, and always wears a boar mask over his head. Because he was raised in the mountains, he has razor-sharp intuition and is highly perceptive.
