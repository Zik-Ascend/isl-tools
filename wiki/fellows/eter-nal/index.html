The founder of Adventurer's Guild, also the current president. He is the hero who defeated the evil monster and sealed it in his sword hundreds of years ago, and he stopped growing ever since, remaining young forever. Now as time passes by, the hero and the monster are no longer hostile to each other. They're now working together to maintain the peace between humans and monsters.
