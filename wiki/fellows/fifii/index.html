Bio

After accidentally obtaining a Wand of Yum, Fifi transformed into a magical chef with the power to cast "deliciousness" onto any food. With her new abilities, she successfully developed the almost imperishable Mega-Delicious Adventurer's Bento, gaining immense popularity among adventurers and becoming their goddess. However, Fifi herself doesn't mind the fame; managing the village restaurant and researching monster cuisine in her spare time is the life she always desired.
