Bio

A crusader born into nobility. At first glance, she looks like a stern, aloof beauty, but in truth, she is a masochist, always eager to rush to the front and serve as a shield for others, enjoying the pleasure of taking pain. That said, her defense is so monstrously high that almost no one can actually hurt her.
