Bio

One of the ancient Magi, she created the energy concentration spell that can make someone hundreds of times more powerful. She is a legendary adventurer, who once cleared a dungeon that took ordinary people months to conquer in just an hour. She casually mentioned that it was all just to quickly enjoy the thrill of defeating the boss. In addition, her unexpectedly enthusiastic and outgoing personality makes her willing to go to great lengths for her friends.
