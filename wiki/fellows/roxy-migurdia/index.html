Bio

A mage from another world and Rudeus's magic tutor. Though she has the appearance of a cute young girl, she's actually a formidable King-Class water mage. When mentoring her students, she's wise and patient; not only does she generously impart the deepest secrets of magic, but she also serves as a vital pillar of emotional support.
