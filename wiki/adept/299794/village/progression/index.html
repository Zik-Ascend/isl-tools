Village progression & earnings

Village staff recruitment, building ranks, business upgrades, attribute enhancement, and earnings bonuses.

How building progression connects

Construction unlocks production. Recruitment increases staff; building ranks raise capacity and add earnings bonuses. Fellow appointments, business levels, and attribute enhancement are separate progression tracks. Farmstead has its own blueprint upgrade track.

Earnings calculation

The client combines base staff yield and Fellow conversion, then applies the ordinary percentage bonuses and the additional benefit-card multiplier. Ordinary sources include building rank, Farmstead, Drakenberg progression, Family-related bonuses, appointment skills, Union Hall, Inn-related bonuses, and chapter targets. Account-specific earnings require the player’s current modifiers.

Prosperity contribution shown by the client: floor((staff × base yield + Fellow conversion) × (1 + ordinary bonus / 10,000) × (1 + extra bonus / 10,000)). Business and attribute values feed their respective property factors; they are not interchangeable with staff counts.
